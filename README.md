# Climate Change Scenarios for Spain and Caldes de Montbui

Spatial analysis of regional climate change scenarios using EURO-CORDEX regional climate models and GIS tools.

## Objective

Assess the potential impacts of climate change on precipitation, temperature and evapotranspiration under different future scenarios.

## Data Sources

- ADAPTECCA
- EURO-CORDEX
- AEMET
- ICGC

## Methodology

1. Selection of representative climate models.
2. Extraction of projected climate variables.
3. Spatial processing using QGIS.
4. Comparison between historical and future periods.
5. Interpretation for water resources and environmental management.

## Variables analysed

- Precipitation
- Temperature
- Potential Evapotranspiration

## Scenarios

- Historical baseline (1971-2000)
- RCP 4.5
- RCP 8.5

## Future periods

- 2011-2040
- 2041-2070
- 2071-2100
